Dizzyits is a small, up-and-coming content creator known for his Minecraft videos, which he edits in DaVinci Resolve, and for dubstep and hardcore music released on SoundCloud.

He makes both long-form and short-form videos, and writes his own music with help from his producer, Alzha Studio. Beyond that, he makes it all for fun.
